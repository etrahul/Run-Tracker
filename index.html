Location & GPS Required

OpenStride requires real GPS location access and phone Location Services turned ON to track your run accurately.

Instructions:

1. Tap Grant / Retry GPS or Open Settings

2. Go to Permissions → Location & Physical Activity

3. Select Allow while using the app & turn ON phone GPS
