OpenStride — Open-Source Architecture & Data Export

MIT Licensed • Self-Contained Browser & PWA Ready

1. Real-Time Geolocation & Haversine Distance Formula

Uses navigator.geolocation.watchPosition with high-accuracy GPS locking and a seamless Live Route Simulator fallback. Distance between coordinate pairs $(\phi_1, \lambda_1)$ and $(\phi_2, \lambda_2)$ is computed via the spherical Haversine formula with Earth radius $R = 6371\text{ km}$.

2. MET Energy Expenditure & Recovery Algorithm

Active calories are computed dynamically via kcal = MET × Weight(kg) × Duration(hr), where MET dynamically scales with live speed (7.0 to 14.0 METs). The Recovery Coach synthesizes daily active burn, sleep duration, and RPE soreness (1–10) into an Energy Bank (0–100%) to prescribe exact exercise minutes/distance and recovery hours.
